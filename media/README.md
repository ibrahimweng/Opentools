# Videos

Upload each tool's demo video in the studio, at **/studio**: open the tool,
then **Video → Demo video**. Publish, and it plays in the tool's pop-up.

- **Format:** MP4 (H.264) plays everywhere.
- **Shape:** 16:10 fills the pop-up exactly. 16:9 works too, trimmed slightly
  at the sides.
- **Length and size:** 10 to 30 seconds and under about 8 MB keeps the pop-up
  quick to open. Nothing loads until the pop-up does.

This folder is only for the starting copy in `assets/js/tools.js`, which the
site falls back to when the studio has nothing published.
