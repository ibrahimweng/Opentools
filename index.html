    About

    Not a subscription.

    Opentools is a growing shelf of tools for the parts of design work that eat the most hours: drawing type, collecting ideas, staging mockups, building icon sets, finding a brand’s patterns, scoring sound to picture and packaging a brand for handover.

    Most of them run entirely in your browser. Your fonts, screenshots and recordings are read on your own machine and never uploaded, and there is no account to make.

    Each one is a single tool that does its job thoroughly, documented as plainly as it is designed.
